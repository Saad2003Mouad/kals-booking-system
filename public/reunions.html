Reunion Ice Cream Truck FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking Boston Legend for your event

Is an ice cream truck a good idea for a family reunion? +

Absolutely! An ice cream truck creates a fun, nostalgic atmosphere that guests of all ages love. It's a unique, memorable touch for any family or class reunion.

How much does an ice cream truck cost for a reunion? +

Reunion packages start at $190 for 30 guests. For larger family or class reunions, packages scale up accordingly. Custom pricing available for 200+ guests.

Can you serve at outdoor parks for reunions? +

Yes! Boston Legend regularly serves at parks, picnic areas, and outdoor venues across Greater Boston. We just need accessible vehicle entry to the location.

How early should I book for a reunion event? +

For summer reunions (JuneAugust), book 46 weeks ahead. For spring or fall reunions, 23 weeks is usually sufficient to secure your date.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions at reunions? +

We carry a range of products. Contact us at 617-999-3803 to discuss specific dietary needs and we'll do our best to accommodate all your guests.
