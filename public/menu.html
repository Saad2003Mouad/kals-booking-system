Common Questions

Boston Legend General FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking Boston Legend for your event

How much does it cost to rent an ice cream truck in Massachusetts? +

Our packages start at $190 for up to 30 guests. Pricing depends on your guest count, event location, and duration. For large events of 200+ guests, we provide custom quotes.

What areas in Massachusetts do you serve? +

We serve 140+ cities across Greater Boston and Massachusetts, including Boston, Cambridge, Somerville, Newton, Brookline, Revere, Quincy, Lynn, and many more.

What types of events do you cater? +

We cater all types of occasions! Birthdays, corporate events, weddings, school field days, block parties, fundraisers, sports tournaments, and photo shoots.

What ice cream flavors do you serve? +

We offer a premium selection of classic and seasonal flavors. Our menu includes novelty bars, ice cream sandwiches, fruit pops, and soft serve options that guests of all ages love.

How far in advance should I book my event? +

For peak season (May to September), we highly recommend booking 2-4 weeks in advance, especially for weekends. Weekday events can usually be accommodated with 1-2 weeks notice.
